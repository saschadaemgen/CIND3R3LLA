What a cadence can say

A destination group and at least one trigger: every N minutes, every N member messages, or both, whichever comes first. Minutes run 1 to 10080, messages 1 to 10000. Nothing applies until you press Set cadence.

What is not settable, on purpose

Track choice on a cadence is shuffle without replacement: every track plays once before any repeats, derived from the plays log. A member asking for a track consults no budget; the budgets under Storage bound only unbidden sends.
