# Season 5 concept notes

**These are proposals, not decisions.** Nothing here may be cited as a decision. What has actually
been adopted is in [`../../docs/decisions.md`](../../docs/decisions.md) with a `D-<n>` number and a
Status, and it is usually narrower than the concept that preceded it. On any divergence the order is
**the code, then the six living documents, then these** - the same rule D-110 set for
[`docs/planning/`](../../docs/planning/), for the same reason: a document that runs ahead of the code
is useful right up until somebody mistakes it for the record.

## Provenance, stated plainly

These two notes were **composed under CCB-S5-003 from that briefing's own itemisation of the Season 5
concept work**, not transcribed from the operator's originals. The briefing says two concept notes
exist and are held outside this repository; they were not available when these were written. Every
figure that could be checked against the code was checked and is cited to its file; everything else is
the briefing's own wording, reorganised.

**If the operator's originals exist, they should replace or be merged into these**, and this paragraph
should be deleted when that happens.

## The notes

- [`avatar-layer.md`](avatar-layer.md) - the three pillars: arming the moderation, the privileged
  moderation channel, and the learning path.
- [`platform-and-reach.md`](platform-and-reach.md) - the request queue, the channel bridge, the
  gallery, the hardware and reachability pages, the smaller carried items, and what is parked.

## Scope

These cover Season 5's *forward* work. What has already been delivered this season is in
[`../SEASON-5-HANDOVER.md`](../SEASON-5-HANDOVER.md), and the authoritative item list, with its
checkboxes and its findings, stays in [`../../docs/feature-backlog.md`](../../docs/feature-backlog.md).
